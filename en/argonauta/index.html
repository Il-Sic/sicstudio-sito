A Sic Studio project

Argonauta

an app to explore, play and dream

Choose a real route and walk. Along the way, places, animals and legends become cards to collect, while your ship, Argo, grows and the heroes of the Argonautica come aboard.

Today Argonauta is in testing, on iPhone and iPad.

Write to me to become a tester

A day with Argonauta

From morning to evening, route after route

Six moments of a day, with real screenshots of the app. The screenshots show the app in Italian.

Morning

Before you set off

Open the route page. In “Before You Set Off” you find sunrise, sunset, when it gets dark and the moon, worked out on the phone, and when you’d arrive if you set off now, with the CAI’s walking time.

On the way

The route follows you

Choose a route and Odyssey shows it on its map. Start it, and from then on the map follows you: if you open another tab, the route in progress stays on top. If you go off route, it tells you, even with the screen locked.

And with your first route, Orpheus the singer comes aboard.

Halfway

A card and its stamp

Along the route and at the arrival, places, animals and plants join your Atlas collection, each with its card and its sources. Every new card gets its stamp, with the route and the day.

At the arrival

A local legend

When you arrive, a legend of the place may be waiting for you. It carries the “Legend” label and says where it comes from.

Evening

The Explorer’s Journal

In the evening, in the Explorer’s Journal, you find your day again: the routes, the stages, the new cards and the heroes who came aboard.

Route after route

Your Argo grows

Every completed route makes your ship grow and takes it to a new stop on the Argonauts’ voyage. The heroes come aboard one at a time.

The ship that grows

Four states, with the routes you complete

The ship grows only with the routes you really complete. Each time it changes, the app tells you once.

  1. 0 routes At the start: the hull and the oars

  2. 1 route Argo has a mast

  3. 3 routes Argo unfurls its sail

  4. 5 routes Argo is complete

The Heroes

The heroes come aboard as you explore

They come from the Argonautica by Apollonius of Rhodes, the Greek poem of Argo’s voyage. Each one comes aboard when you do something, and says a line of their own.

  1. Orpheus the singer

    Comes aboard when you start your first route.

    “Stop for a moment: the woods have their own music too.”

    Apollonius of Rhodes, Argonautica, book I, lines 23-34 and the departure from Pagasae; book IV, the Sirens.

  2. Tiphys the helmsman

    Comes aboard when you complete your first route.

    “Every route you finish is a harbour reached. Where to next?”

    Apollonius of Rhodes, Argonautica, book I, lines 105-110 and the helm, line 394 onwards.

  3. Mopsus the seer

    Comes aboard when the golden eagle joins your collection.

    “Look up, without a sound: birds speak to those who can wait.”

    Apollonius of Rhodes, Argonautica, book I, lines 65-66 and the halcyon, line 1079 onwards.

  4. Lynceus the sharp-eyed

    Comes aboard when you have added six new cards to your collection.

    “I see far: more cards are waiting for you along the way.”

    Apollonius of Rhodes, Argonautica, book I, lines 151-155; book IV, Heracles seen from afar.

  5. Heracles the strongest

    Comes aboard when you have completed five routes.

    “Five routes! Now that is a labour.”

    Apollonius of Rhodes, Argonautica, book I, the oar and the pine, line 1153 onwards; Diodorus Siculus, Library of History, IV, 22, 5-6.

In all there are fifty-six heroes, with Jason and Medea: the others come aboard later.

The myth of Argo, with its sources

Out on the trail, with care

What the app really does

Argonauta helps you find your way, but it isn’t a rescue service. It never says whether a trail is open or in good condition, and it gives no weather or news about refuges or water: it would have no up-to-date source for that. Here’s what it does.

  • “Before You Set Off”, on the route page: sunrise, sunset, dark and the moon, worked out on the phone, and when you’d arrive if you set off now, with the CAI’s walking time.
  • Alerts along the route: if you go off route, it tells you, even with the screen locked.
  • The “Safety” sheet: “Call 112” on iPhone, your location with coordinates to read out, what to do with no signal, and how to get ready.

Privacy in short

What you do stays yours

Argonauta doesn't collect personal data. There's no account. There are no analytics, ads or tracking. What you do in the app stays on your iPhone or iPad, and we don't see it.

The privacy policy

For parks, towns and schools

Your area inside Argonauta

A park, a town or a school can bring into Argonauta the routes, places and stories of its area, each with its sources. They become part of the same app, with the same rules of care.

  1. You send trails, places, texts and stories, with their sources.

  2. I write the cards, the stages and the stories.

  3. You approve every text and every route.

  4. Argonauta brings them to those who explore, in Italian and in English.

If you’d like to talk about it, write to vincenzo@sicstudio.app.

Support

Write to me

For a problem or a question

Write to vincenzo@sicstudio.app. To help me understand, tell me:

  • your iPhone or iPad model and your iOS version, which you’ll find in Settings › General › About;
  • what you were doing and what happened, step by step;
  • if you can, a screenshot.

Many answers are already in Help and questions.

Where it works

  • iPhone and iPad with iOS 26.1 or later.
  • On iPhone the app stays in portrait; on iPad it also turns to landscape.
  • In Italian and in English, following the device’s language.
  • Wi‑Fi‑only iPads don’t have GPS: your location may be missing or inaccurate.
