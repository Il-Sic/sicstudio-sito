Argonauta

“an app to explore, play and dream”

It goes with you to real places: it guides you along the routes, tells you about what you meet, remembers every outing and grows with you. Every outing adds a discovery to your story as an explorer.

Support Help and questions

Today Argonauta is in testing, on iPhone and iPad. The screenshots on this page show the app in Italian.

Guides you

Odyssey, the map and the routes

In Odyssey you see the routes on the map and pick one. Before you set off you can see it whole, with its stages, its kilometres and its elevation profile. The map is inside the app and opens even offline.

When you start the route, Argonauta follows you from stage to stage, even with the screen locked. It tells you when you reach a stage and when you go off route, and the Lock Screen shows you the next stage.

Tells you

The Atlas, myths and legends, Argo

The Atlas collects places, animals, plants and history: every card has its source. Cards can always be read, and routes add them to your collection.

Along some routes Argo tells you a myth or a legend of the place, with the label “Myth” or “Legend” and its source. And if you have a question, ask Argo: it answers from the cards of the Atlas.

Remembers

The Explorer's Journal, badges and levels

The Explorer's Journal keeps your story, day by day: the routes you started and completed, the stages, the new cards, the heroes who come aboard.

Every card that joins your collection gets its stamp: the day and the route on which you found it.

Badges come with routes, missions and new cards. Every stage you reach and every route you complete bring experience points, and with the points a level with a name, from Apprentice to Argonaut.

Grows with you

The ship, the Heroes, the voyage

On Argo's cover there's your ship, and it grows only with routes you really complete. The heroes of the Argonautica come aboard, and every route you complete brings Argo to the next landfall, toward the golden fleece.

Nothing left to chance and no hurry: if the app stays closed, the ship waits for you as it was.

The ship that grows

Four states, with the routes you complete

The ship grows only with the routes you really complete. Each time it changes, the app tells you once.

  1. 0 routes

    At the start: the hull and the oars

  2. 1 route

    Argo has a mast

  3. 3 routes

    Argo unfurls its sail

  4. 5 routes

    Argo is complete

The Heroes

The heroes of the Argonautica come aboard your Argo as you explore

Each one comes aboard with something you really did: a route started or completed, a card discovered. Aboard they live their own lives, and at night they all sleep, except Tiphys at the helm.

  • Orpheus the singer

    Comes aboard when you start your first route.

    Apollonius of Rhodes, Argonautica 1.23–34

  • Tiphys the helmsman

    Comes aboard when you complete your first route.

    Apollonius of Rhodes, Argonautica 1.105–110

  • Mopsus the seer

    Comes aboard when the golden eagle joins your collection.

    Apollonius of Rhodes, Argonautica 1.65–66

  • Lynceus the sharp-eyed

    Comes aboard when you have added six new cards to your collection.

    Apollonius of Rhodes, Argonautica 1.151–155

  • Heracles the strongest

    Comes aboard when you have completed five routes.

    Apollonius of Rhodes, Argonautica 1.1153 and following

In the Heroes sheet there are also the other Argonauts from Apollonius of Rhodes' catalogue, each with their myth and its source. Every story carries the label “Myth”.

Why Argo: the myth

Out on the trail, with care

What the app really does

Argonauta helps you find your way, but it isn't a rescue service. It never says whether a trail is open or in good condition, and it gives no weather or news about refuges or water: it would have no up-to-date source for that. Here's what it does.

  • “Before You Set Off”, on the route page: sunrise, sunset, dark and the moon, worked out on the phone, and when you'd arrive if you set off now, with the CAI's walking time.
  • Alerts along the route: if you go off route, it tells you, even with the screen locked.
  • The “Safety” sheet: “Call 112” on iPhone, your location with coordinates to read out, what to do with no signal, and how to get ready.
Privacy in short

What you do stays yours

Argonauta doesn't collect personal data. There's no account. There are no analytics, ads or tracking. What you do in the app stays on your iPhone or iPad, and we don't see it.

For parks, towns and schools

Your area inside Argonauta

A park, a town or a school can bring into Argonauta the routes, places and stories of its area, each with its sources. They become part of the same app, with the same rules of care. If you'd like to talk about it, write to vincenzo@sicstudio.app.

Support

Write to us

For a problem or a question

Write to vincenzo@sicstudio.app. To help us understand, tell us:

  • your iPhone or iPad model and your iOS version, which you'll find in Settings › General › About;
  • what you were doing and what happened, step by step;
  • if you can, a screenshot.

Many answers are already in Help and questions.

Where it works

  • iPhone and iPad with iOS 26.1 or later.
  • On iPhone the app stays in portrait; on iPad it also turns to landscape.
  • In Italian and in English, following the device's language.
  • Wi‑Fi‑only iPads don't have GPS: your location may be missing or inaccurate.
