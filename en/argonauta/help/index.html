Before you go

How can I try it?

Argonauta is in testing with TestFlight, Apple’s free app for trying apps before they come out. Write to me at vincenzo@sicstudio.app and I’ll send you an invitation.

Do I need a network connection?

Not for walking. The map, the , the navigation, the and are inside the app and work even without signal.

You need the network only for Apple’s “Hybrid” and “Satellite” maps, to search on those maps, and for a layer of places that is off at the start.

Which location permission does it ask for? Why never “Always”?

Argonauta asks for your location only “While Using the App”, never “Always”. When you start a route it keeps following you even with the screen locked, and iOS shows it with the location indicator.

When you pause, exit the route or arrive, it stops following you. To follow a route you also need “Precise Location”.

Which devices does it work on?

On iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26.1 or later. On iPhone the app stays in portrait; on iPad it turns in every direction, with the panel on the left, as in Maps.

Can I walk with an iPad?

Wi‑Fi‑only iPads don’t have GPS: on the trail, use an iPhone or a Wi‑Fi + Cellular iPad. When your location is missing or inaccurate, the app says so.

On iPad there’s no “Call 112” button, because iPad doesn’t make phone calls.

On the trail

What happens if I leave the route?

If you stay more than 80 metres from the route for at least 30 seconds, Argonauta tells you once: with the navigation open the phone vibrates, with the screen locked you get the notification “You’re off route”, with your distance from the route.

On the map the line turns orange until you’re back. A position off the route doesn’t move the route forward and doesn’t complete .

Which notifications does it send? And the Live Activity?

Notifications are created on the phone, only during a route and only when the app isn’t in front: “Stage reached”, “You’ve arrived” and “You’re off route”. iOS asks for permission the first time you tap “Start”.

On the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, the Live Activity shows the next stage, how far it is and how much of the route you’ve walked.

Why doesn’t the app say whether a trail is open?

Because trail conditions change, and the app has no up-to-date source to know them. That’s why it never says whether a trail is open or in good condition, or whether water is drinkable.

, ask whoever manages the protected area or the CAI, the Italian Alpine Club, and check an official weather service.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call 112: it’s the single emergency number. Argonauta isn’t a rescue service, but in the “” sheet you’ll find “Call 112”, your coordinates to read to the operator, and “Send My Location”.

If there’s no signal, on iPhone 14 and later call 112 anyway: if the call doesn’t go through, iPhone offers to text emergency services via satellite.

What’s in “Before You Set Off”?

On every route page, the app works out on the phone, even offline, the sunrise, sunset, dark and the moon. With the CAI’s walking time it tells you when you’d arrive if you set off now, and warns you if you’d arrive after sunset.

Below there are three tips, “Tell Someone Your Plans” and the “Safety” sheet.

The Atlas and Argo

How does a card join my collection?

The of the Atlas can all be read, always. A card “To discover” joins “In your collection” when you do something real in Odyssey: you start or finish a route, or reach one of its stages. Some are there from the start.

For animals and plants, discovering the card doesn’t mean you’ve seen them: the card comes with the route.

Why doesn’t Argo answer everything?

Argo answers only with what’s in the cards of the Atlas, and under the answer it shows you the card it comes from. If the cards don’t say it, it answers “I can’t find it in the Atlas cards.”.

On emergencies, health, mushrooms and plants to eat, drinking water, trails and weather it makes nothing up: it always gives the same answer, and for emergencies it points you to 112.

What are “Myths and Legends”?

Tales handed down in a real place, which Argo tells you when you arrive. The app always marks them as “Myth” or “Legend”, cites the source and never presents them as history.

I found a mistake in a card.

Every card says which sources it comes from. If something doesn’t add up, write to vincenzo@sicstudio.app with the name of the card: there’s no form in the app to report it.

The ship and the voyage

How does the ship grow?

With the routes you really complete: the hull with the oars at the start, the mast at the first route, the sail unfurled at the third, the complete ship at the fifth.

Nothing left to chance and no hurry: if the app stays closed, the ship waits for you as it was.

Who comes aboard?

The heroes of Apollonius of Rhodes’ Argonautica, each with something you really did: Orpheus when you start your first route, Tiphys when you complete it, Mopsus when the golden eagle joins your collection, Lynceus with six new cards, Heracles with five routes.

In the sheet there are also the other Argonauts, each with their myth and its source.

What is the Voyage of Argo?

It’s the of the Argonautica. Argo sets off from Pagasae, and every route you complete brings it to the next landfall: Lemnos, Cyzicus, the Clashing Rocks, Colchis and, at the fifth route, the Golden Fleece. Every landfall has its episode, with the label “Myth” and its source.

Read the myth of Argo

How do badges and levels work?

Every stage you reach and every route you complete give experience points. The are called Apprentice, Wayfarer, Seafarer, Lookout, Commander and Argonaut.

Badges are gold medals you earn with routes, and new cards.

Data and language

How do I delete my data?

“The data on your device is yours alone: to delete it, just remove the app.”

Read the privacy policy

Is the app in English?

Yes. Argonauta speaks Italian and English and follows your device’s language: it’s in Italian on a device in Italian, and in English in every other language.

The cards, the stages, the heroes and the voyage are in English too; proper names and quoted sources stay as they are.

Does it work with Siri and Shortcuts?

With Siri and Shortcuts you can open Argo’s sheet, the Atlas or a card by name, for example “Open Apennine wolf in Argonauta”. The cards also show up in iPhone search.

Argo, though, always answers in its own sheet, inside the app.

Words on board

The app’s words, one for each thing.

Route
The way to walk, from the start to the finish, with its stages.
Stage
A point on the route: the start, the middle, the finish. You reach it by passing within 45 metres.
Mission
What the game counts: every stage you reach is a mission completed.
Odyssey
The map tab, with the routes, the stages and the navigation.
Atlas
The collection of cards: Places, Fauna, Flora and History.
Card
A place, an animal, a plant or a story, with its sources. You can always read it.
Explorer’s Journal
The day-by-day story of what you did in Odyssey.
The collection stamp
The blue circle, slightly tilted, that a card gets when it joins your collection, with the route and the date.
Badge
A gold medal for a milestone: routes, missions, new cards.
Level
It grows with the experience points of stages and routes; it has a name, from Apprentice to Argonaut.
Argo
Your ship, on the app’s cover, and the guide that answers from the cards of the Atlas.
Heroes
The heroes of the Argonautica who come aboard your Argo as you explore.
The Voyage of Argo
From Pagasae to the Golden Fleece: a landfall for every route you complete.
Myths and Legends
Tales handed down in real places, with their label and source, never told as history.
Before You Set Off
On the route page: daylight, the moon, your arrival time with the CAI’s walking time, and tips.
Safety
The sheet with “Call 112”, your location and what to do with no signal.
