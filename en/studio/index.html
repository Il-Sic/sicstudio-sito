The studio

Sic Studio is Vincenzo Siciliano’s studio, in Calabria. It makes things for going outdoors and raising your eyes.

Why Argonauta

I make Argonauta so that everyone can have fun, explore and find their own way.

Life can sometimes be an odyssey, or truly hard like the Argonautica, but you must always persevere: on the journey of life, if we raised our eyes and looked around us, we would see so many beautiful things we might have missed.

I picture an app that everyone can use, grown-ups and little ones alike, one that helps them find the love and the joy of going out and exploring, like young pirates in search of treasure: knowledge and curiosity.

Vincenzo Siciliano

The name

sic
Latin adverb “thus”.

“Sic” comes from Siciliano, my surname. In Latin it means “thus”.

It is also the word written in square brackets, [sic], when a sentence is quoted just as it is in its source, even with a mistake. For a studio that always says where things come from, it fits.

The manifesto

  1. We cannot know where life will take us, but we can choose not to go through it as spectators.
  2. The Greeks had a word, pothos: the longing to discover the unknown.
  3. We make things for going outdoors and raising our eyes, for grown-ups and little ones.
  4. The treasure is knowledge; curiosity is the map.
  5. Every fact has its source, and a myth stays a myth.
  6. Better a few things done well than many left half-done.

How we work

Four kinds of care, the same in everything the studio makes.

  1. We say where things come from

    The Atlas cards, the myths and the stops of Argo’s voyage say where they come from: a book, an official website, an ancient work with its book and lines.

  2. A myth stays a myth

    A myth or a legend always carries the label “Myth” or “Legend” and its source. We never tell it as if it were history.

  3. Caution first

    Argonauta never says that a trail is open or in good condition: it would have no up-to-date source for that. For emergencies there is “Call 112”.

  4. What you do stays yours

    “Argonauta doesn't collect personal data. There's no account. There are no analytics, ads or tracking. What you do in the app stays on your iPhone or iPad, and we don't see it.”
